About Digital Journal

Welcome to your personal Digital Journal – a beautiful space to capture your thoughts, feelings, and memories.

Features:

  • Daily Entries: Write and organize your daily journal entries with titles, content, and tags.
  • Mood Tracker: Track your emotional journey with mood logging and visual statistics.
  • Photo Memories: Store and cherish your favorite moments with photos and captions.
  • Search & Filter: Easily find entries by tags or keywords.
  • Local Storage: All your data is stored locally and kept private.

How to Use:

  1. Click "New Entry" to create a journal entry
  2. Select your mood to track your emotional state
  3. Upload photos to create visual memories
  4. Search through your entries anytime
